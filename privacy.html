Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026. Three Days is an independent household preparedness app by Adam Jones.

On your iPhone

Your household, supplies, stock dates, medicines, baby routines, contacts, checks, packing lists and household plan (including photos) are stored locally. Three Days has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or developer-operated server. It does not send this household database to the developer.

iPhone or iCloud device backups may include the app's data according to your Apple settings. Three Days does not offer live iCloud household syncing.

Optional online services

Online requests go directly from your phone to each provider. Providers receive your IP address and the requested information, and may retain service or security logs under their policies. Local storage does not mean that these requests are anonymous.

  • Postcode lookup: your postcode goes to postcodes.io. The returned coordinates go to police.uk for police force and neighbourhood details. Results are stored on the phone. You can skip lookup and select your nation. Lookup is available for England, Scotland and Wales; Northern Ireland uses nation selection.
  • Product lookup: only the barcode goes to Open Food Facts for product and nutrition suggestions. Camera processing stays on the phone; no camera frames are uploaded. Manual product entry works offline. Check suggestions against your pack.
  • Warnings: requests to the Met Office contain the selected weather region. The Environment Agency provides an England flood feed; council and county matching happens on your phone. Warnings are refreshed while using the app, not delivered as an emergency push-alert service.
  • External links open the selected website, which follows its own privacy and cookie policies.

Permissions and reminders

Camera permission is optional and used for barcode scanning. Plan photos are chosen through the system photo picker and saved locally. Notification permission is optional and used for local stock and check reminders. You can change these permissions in iPhone Settings.

Exports, sharing and deletion

Backup and household handover files may include sensitive medicine information, contacts and plan photos. You choose where to save or share them. Three Days does not add its own encryption to exported files. Treat them as private. Import replaces the local household snapshot after review and keeps a local recovery copy.

Settings → Delete everything removes local household records, the recovery copy and pending reminders. It cannot remove exported files, messages or backups stored elsewhere. Manage those separately.

Contact

Email privacy@audishly.app for privacy questions. This is the developer's shared app-support domain. If you email, your email address and anything you include are handled to answer your request. Avoid sending household files or medical information unless necessary and deliberately chosen. We do not sell personal information.
