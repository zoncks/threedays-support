Three Days support

A little more ready. A personal checklist and supply organiser for UK households.

Three Days is independent and isn't government advice. In an emergency, call 999. Always check official advice.

Getting started

Set up your household, review your tailored kit and record what you already have. Quantities labelled as starting estimates are editable. Choose the hygiene, baby, pet and medicine products your household actually uses. You can mark personal checklist items complete without recording stock.

Stock and reminders

Record separate lots when dates or storage places differ. Pantry product scanning is optional; manual entry works without a camera or internet connection. Nutrition results are suggestions to check against the pack. Medicines never calculate a dose. Enable notifications in both the app and iPhone Settings for local reminders.

Warnings and offline use

Saved kit, plan and guides work offline. Live warnings and product or postcode lookups need internet. Weather warnings are regional; England flood matches use council/county names. The app does not provide postcode emergency push alerts or an all-clear. Use the official services for current information.

Postcode lookup is optional for England, Scotland and Wales. In Northern Ireland, select your nation for official links and use the rest of the app without a postcode.

Backup and household handover

Use Settings → Backup and household handover to save a file. Import shows a preview before replacing local information. It is a point-in-time snapshot, not live household syncing. A recovery copy is retained locally when replacing a household. Keep your own exported backups somewhere secure.

Contact the developer

Email hello@audishly.app with “Three Days” in the subject, your app version (Settings → Privacy and support), iOS version and a short description. This is the developer's shared app-support inbox. Avoid attaching household files, postcodes, medicine details or plan photos unless you choose to share them.
